Plain — a heading and description in the flow; the questions keep their own cards.

Card — one frame around the run; the questions inside lose their own frames and are separated by rules, so the group reads as one block.

Collapsible — the card with its header as a button (44 px tap target, chevron, aria-expanded). Starts closed and shows "3 questions" so the respondent knows what is inside. Press Next while it is closed: the required question is unanswered, so the group opens and the error is shown (spec: "Collapsed group with an unanswered required question").

The question count appears only in the collapsible style.

2 · Geo pop-up (epic #343 target picture)

Point question "My places" — Type of place (icon grid), then the group "Your visit" ruled by "Type of place answered"

Your visit 3 questions

About the last time you were here.

Pick a type: "Your visit" appears (acceptance scenario of #340). Clear it by reloading.

In the pop-up the popup itself is the frame, so ungrouped questions stay frameless (as today). A card group is the only framed thing inside, which is what makes the block readable among the other follow-ups; plain is only a heading between rules.

Hidden groups keep their DOM; inputs are disabled so nothing posts (same mechanism as sub-question rules, #338).

A group row is tinted indigo, with an object-group icon, Group · N, its rule and its comment badge. The second line is the display style and the first line of the description.

The member list is a drop zone (the faded row is being dragged in). An empty group shows "Drag questions here", so the zone can be found. Groups move only within the top list or the pop-up list and can never be dropped into another group.

"Add group" sits next to "New Question" and next to "Add Sub-question", at the same weight.

Click 🗑 on "Your visit" to open the delete dialog (§5).

5 · Delete a group (decided 2026-10-19: ask)

Delete group “Your visit”?

12 answers will be destroyed. This survey has no earlier version to keep them.

"Only the group" is the default. Choosing "with its questions" changes the button to Delete group and questions. The answer count is shown after the server's 409 (the existing warn-before-destroying-answers flow, here inlined into the dialog), never pre-rendered. An empty group gets the plain one-line browser confirm.
