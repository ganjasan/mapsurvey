Behaviour notes (not part of the UI)
  • Shows on /editor/ only when the workspace has ≥1 survey AND no connected app / personal token with first use. Never on respondent pages.
  • The example chips are built from the creator's own survey names (first two published surveys), so the text is about their work, not a demo.
  • × hides it for good (CreatorPreferences.agent_nudge_dismissed_at); connecting hides it by itself. One nudge per creator, not per workspace.
  • "Connect your assistant" → /editor/surveys/start/agent/?from=dashboard; "How it works" → help centre MCP guide. Events: agent_nudge_shown, agent_nudge_clicked, agent_nudge_dismissed.
  • Free workspace: the same banner, the line under the buttons says "Part of Pro · connecting is free" — connecting works, building needs Pro (the yellow strip on the agent page handles the upsell).
