        Spot 1

        What the section context forces on top of the single-question design: ① only one geo question can be armed at a time — arming another disarms the first, and the map badge always names the question being answered, with its colour; ② counter, progress and feature list are per question, never section-wide; ③ each popup shows that question's own sub-questions (q1 asks frequency/cause, q2 asks a rating, q3 asks nothing at all — an anonymous polygon layer); ④ lists collapse past 3 items, otherwise three geo questions × 5 features push the ordinary questions off the panel; ⑤ a question with max = 1 keeps today's look — no chip, no progress, no list clutter (q3); ⑥ the legend makes it obvious the marks are different layers, not one pile.
