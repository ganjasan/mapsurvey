What's new in Mapsurvey

New Oct 6, 2026

Empty sessions are now hidden

Responses, Overview, Map and exports now leave out sessions where nobody answered anything. Most of them were people opening the link and leaving, and on busy surveys they outnumbered real responses 3 to 1. The headline shows how many were hidden, and you can switch them back on at any time.

Why: creators were deleting these rows one by one, and clients asked "how many people actually responded?" with no clear number to point at. The section funnel on the Perf tab still counts everyone who opened the survey.

Export responses as Excel, GeoPackage, Shapefile or KML

The Export dialog on the Responses page now offers five formats. GeoPackage and Shapefile keep every geo question as its own layer, with the sub-question answers as attributes.
