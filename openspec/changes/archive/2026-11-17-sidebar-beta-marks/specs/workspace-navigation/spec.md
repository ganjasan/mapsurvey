## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Modules in beta are marked
The sidebar SHALL show the letter "β" beside the label of every module the navigation registry marks as beta — Data and Reports — and beside no other row. The mark SHALL carry the word "Beta" for a pointer and for assistive technology, and SHALL NOT be shown in the icon rail.

#### Scenario: Data and Reports
- **WHEN** a member opens the dashboard
- **THEN** the Data and Reports rows carry the beta mark, and Surveys, Sites and the other rows do not
