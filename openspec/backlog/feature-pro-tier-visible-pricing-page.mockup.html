Questions

Before you forward this to finance

Is there a per-respondent, per-survey or per-seat charge?

No. A survey with 40 responses and one with 4,000 cost the same. Members are unlimited on Workspace.

Can we pay by invoice?

Yes. Annual Workspace and Project are invoiced; monthly Workspace is by card. Purchase orders are fine.

Where is the data hosted?

Free and Workspace run on our shared instance in the United States (Render, media in AWS). On a Project licence you choose: EU, US, or a dedicated instance under your own data-processing agreement. Tell us what your procurement requires and we quote it in the same offer.

We are a university group or a community initiative.

Stay on Free. It is the same tool. If you need a public results page for a thesis or a neighbourhood campaign, write to us. We usually say yes in exchange for a short case study.

What happens to our data if we stop paying?

Nothing. Surveys keep collecting, exports keep working. Public pages and extra members are paused until you resume.

Can we self-host?

Yes, the code is AGPLv3 on GitHub. Self-hosting includes none of the support above.
