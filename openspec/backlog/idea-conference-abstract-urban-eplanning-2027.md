## Description

Submit an abstract to the 11th International Conference on Urban e-Planning (5–9 April 2027, online, hosted from Lisbon; proceedings feed the IJEPR journal). Track 3, "Citizens e-Participation in Urban Governance". Working title: *"From creation to data collection: where the self-service PPGIS survey path breaks — observations from 373 accounts on a self-service platform"*. A descriptive study of the creator funnel on Mapsurvey, which no PPGIS vendor publishes and which the academic literature (instrument set up by a university or consultant per project) cannot see.

## Notes

**Funnel (prod DB, 2026-09-14, all-time, versions merged, draft/testing excluded):**

| Stage | Users / surveys |

The biggest drop is created → published, not published → answered.

**Sections planned:** (1) funnel; (2) who reaches the end — municipalities & parish councils, university research, civic groups running a series of surveys; students and one-off visitors drop at draft; (3) respondent drop-off on map input (Skelozard: 124 arrivals, 45 placed a point); (4) AI drafting lowers the "where do I start" barrier (19 of 21 recent publications from an AI draft) but not the distribution barrier. Framed honestly as descriptive; proposed next step is interviews with 10 "created, never published" authors.
