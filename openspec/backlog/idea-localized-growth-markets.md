# Localized growth in markets with existing traction

**Type**: idea
**Priority**: low
**Area**: general
**Epic**: growth
**Created**: 2026-06-10

## Description

Concentrate growth effort on the countries/languages where the platform already shows organic traction, rather than spraying globally. Localize the acquisition surface (landing page, onboarding, the viral-loop CTA) in those languages and double down where clusters already form.

## Evidence (from 2026-06-10 analysis)

Growth clusters geographically:

- **Indonesia** — FTSPK class (largest cluster, ~30 creators)
- **Netherlands** — Dutch Vriendenweekend viral cluster, several Dutch registrants
- **France** — Lyon transit, Ivry mobility
- **Italy** — Decisio consultancy, Univ. of Milan, CNR
- **Germany** — Berlin Senate, TU Dortmund, multiple registrants

## Scope

- Localize landing + onboarding + viral-loop CTA copy in: ID, NL, FR, IT, DE (the platform already supports 75 survey-content languages and EN/RU UI chrome).
- Per-market light positioning: lean on the locally relevant use case (mobility in FR, participatory planning in IT/DE, coursework in ID).
- Surface localized [survey templates](feature-survey-template-gallery.md) per market.

## Notes

- Enabler dependency: [UI i18n](feature-ui-i18n.md) (translate UI buttons/instructions) — distinct from this GTM idea, which is about *acquisition copy and market focus*, not the in-app UI strings.
- Lowest priority of the growth epic — sequence after coursework (#1), viral loop (#2), and template gallery (#3) prove out.
- Don't over-invest before [funnel monitoring](feature-funnel-monitoring.md) confirms which markets convert, not just which register.
