{% trans "What it is" %}

{% trans "What a map-based community engagement platform does" %}

{% trans "A community engagement platform is the software a council, a city department, an agency or a consultancy uses to collect public input online and turn it into something a committee can act on. Most platforms are built around pages and forms: a project page, a survey, a comment box, a report. A map-based platform starts from a different question. Where? Where is the crossing unsafe, which street needs lighting, which route do you cycle, which corner of the park should stay as it is. People answer by marking the place, and the answer arrives with coordinates attached." %}

{% trans "That changes what the team receives. A free-text comment that says 'the junction by the school is dangerous' has to be read, interpreted and geocoded by hand before it can be counted. A pin on that junction, with a follow-up question about why, is already a data point. Fifty of them are a cluster. Five hundred are evidence for a capital bid. This is what makes map-based community engagement a planning tool rather than a mailbox: the same software that collects the input also produces the map, the counts and the export the GIS team needs." %}

{% trans "Local government teams call the same thing a public engagement platform, public participation software or public involvement software, depending on the country and the statute. Consultancies call it stakeholder engagement. Universities call it participatory GIS. Mapsurvey serves all of them with one survey builder: ordinary question types for the people, places and preferences, and map question types for the where. Each survey has a public link that opens on any phone, needs no account, and can be embedded in the project page you already have." %}

{% trans "The platform is open source under the AGPLv3 licence. The hosted version runs on servers in the United States today; a team that needs the data to stay inside its own jurisdiction runs the same Docker stack on its own infrastructure, at no licence cost, and a choice of hosting region is on the Pro roadmap. Either way the data is yours: every response exports, every survey can be deleted, and nothing is locked behind a tier." %}
