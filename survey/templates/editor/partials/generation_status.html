{% comment %} Polled while a draft is being generated. Rendered as a fixed overlay rather than inline in the form: generation takes up to a minute and the creator must not miss that it started, nor keep editing a form whose values are already on their way to the model. Inline it also sat below the fold on a laptop, which is exactly the confusion this replaces. The wait is presented as a progress bar, and the rule that has governed this card since the quips era is unchanged: nothing here may imply a pipeline stage the backend does not have. The bar honours it in both phases — indeterminate (animated stripe) while the model is reasoning and there is genuinely nothing to measure, determinate once questions start closing, filled from counts of what the model has actually written (see generation_progress.html for the denominator's honesty rules). The quips were removed by explicit product decision, 2026-08-17: creators should see the process, not flavour text. {% endcomment %}
