{% trans "What it is" %}

{% trans "What a citizen engagement platform is, and what the map adds" %}

{% trans "A citizen engagement platform is the software a local government uses to involve residents in its decisions online: a consultation on a road scheme, a survey about a park, a call for ideas for the town centre, a participatory budget. Vendors also call it a citizen participation platform, citizen engagement software for local government, or a resident engagement platform; the job is the same. Residents are invited, they respond, and the council turns the responses into a report, a plan or a vote at committee." %}

{% trans "Most platforms are built around pages and forms. A map-based citizen engagement platform starts from the question local decisions actually turn on: where. Which crossing is unsafe, which street floods, which route do people cycle, which corner of the park should stay as it is. On Mapsurvey residents answer by marking the place, and each mark carries the follow-up questions you attach to it. A comment that says 'the junction by the school is dangerous' has to be read, interpreted and geocoded by an officer before it can be counted. A pin on that junction is already a data point; five hundred of them are evidence for a capital bid." %}

{% trans "That is also why the data comes out in a form a council can use. Every response exports with its geometry as GeoJSON, GeoPackage, Shapefile or KML for the GIS team, and as CSV or Excel for everyone else. The responses map inside the platform is the working view for the project officer: filter by question and answer, switch to a heat map, open any single response, clean out the test runs. A public results page shows residents that their input was counted." %}

{% trans "Mapsurvey is open source under the AGPLv3 licence. The hosted service runs on servers in the United States today; a council whose policy requires the data to stay inside its own jurisdiction runs the same Docker stack on its own infrastructure, at no licence cost, and a choice of hosting region is on the Pro roadmap. Either way the data is yours: every response exports, every survey can be deleted, and nothing is locked behind a tier." %}
