Mapsurvey vs Maptionnaire

Both platforms let you build map-based surveys where participants mark places, draw routes, and outline areas. The difference is in pricing model, licensing, and data sovereignty.

TL;DR

  • Choose Maptionnaire if you need a mature commercial product with bundled premium support, have budget for per-survey pricing, and prefer a fully managed platform with a long track record in Nordic participatory planning
  • Choose Mapsurvey if you need open-source transparency, want to self-host for data sovereignty, or run multiple surveys without multiplying costs

Choosing between them

Choose Maptionnaire if:

  • You value a mature commercial product with a long track record in Nordic participatory planning
  • Your organization's procurement prefers a Finnish/EU commercial vendor over open-source
  • You're already running on Maptionnaire and switching cost outweighs the savings
  • You need features currently on Mapsurvey's roadmap (custom WMS/WFS basemaps, Shapefile export) today

Choose Mapsurvey if:

  • You need open-source transparency for audit, academic publication, or institutional policy
  • You want to self-host for data sovereignty or air-gapped environments
  • You run many surveys and per-survey pricing adds up fast
  • Your budget for GIS tooling is tight — research groups, NGOs, small municipalities
