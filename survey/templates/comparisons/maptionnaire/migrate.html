Migration Guide

Migrate from Maptionnaire to Mapsurvey

A practical, step-by-step guide for teams switching participatory mapping platforms. Export your data, recreate surveys, and keep running — usually in under a day.

Migration steps

1

Export your data from Maptionnaire

Download survey responses and geographic features from your Maptionnaire admin. If you have ongoing surveys, run the export during a quiet period — respondents who submit after your export won't appear in the file.

2

Prepare your data

Geographic features export cleanly to GeoJSON in WGS84 (EPSG:4326) — the format Mapsurvey uses natively. Attribute responses (text, choice, numeric) map directly to Mapsurvey question types. For multilingual surveys, verify each language variant is preserved in its own column.

3

Create your Mapsurvey account

Sign up at mapsurvey.org — free, no credit card. Create an organization if you want to collaborate with colleagues.

4

Recreate your survey

In the Mapsurvey editor, rebuild your questionnaire structure: create a new survey, add sections matching your Maptionnaire pages, add questions across 13 available types (text, choice, rating, point/line/polygon, image, and more), configure translations if your survey is multilingual. For a 20–30 question survey, expect 30–60 minutes in the editor.

What's different

You'll need to adjust:

  • URLs change — respondent links will point to mapsurvey.org/surveys/<slug> or your self-hosted domain
  • If you used custom CSS / branding on Maptionnaire, plan design time on Mapsurvey — we have editor settings for basic branding; deeper customization requires template edits on self-hosted installs
  • Analytics dashboards look different — Mapsurvey groups analytics under one survey with a stats sidebar, filter panel, and map view. The IDE-style panel layout (map, table, charts, anomalies) is part of the v2 data management release
  • PDF reports — Mapsurvey does not currently generate PDF reports; export to CSV/GeoJSON and analyze in QGIS, R, or Python instead

You'll gain:

  • No per-survey fees — run as many surveys as you want
  • Source code access — audit the platform, fork it, or self-host
  • Survey versioning — publish a draft alongside your live survey without breaking respondent links
  • Standard open data formats (GeoJSON, CSV) with no vendor-specific fields

FAQ

How long does a migration take?

For a single survey with 20–30 questions: half a day to rebuild the structure in the editor, plus your own testing time. For an organization with 5+ active surveys: plan 2–3 days.

Can we run both platforms in parallel during migration?

Yes. Keep your Maptionnaire subscription active while you test Mapsurvey. When confident, redirect new respondents to Mapsurvey and let your Maptionnaire surveys wind down.

Is there a bulk import tool for Maptionnaire data?

Not out of the box. For large migrations (hundreds of surveys or millions of responses), contact us and we can help write an import script. For standard projects, manual rebuild in the editor is faster than scripting.

Is my data safe during the transition?

Yes — Mapsurvey data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt), encrypted in transit (TLS), and fully exportable at any time. See our Trust page for details.

What if we want to switch back?

Mapsurvey exports everything as GeoJSON + CSV — standard formats any platform can import. No vendor lock-in, no switching fee.
