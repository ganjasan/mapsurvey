Mapsurvey vs Maptionnaire

A free, open-source Maptionnaire alternative

Mapsurvey is a participatory mapping platform with no per-survey pricing, full data export, and optional self-hosting. Same core job as Maptionnaire — different economics.

Why teams choose Mapsurvey

  • Free to start — no per-survey fees, no seat licensing. Maptionnaire's lowest tier is $950 USD/month (Kyttä et al., 2023)
  • Open source (AGPLv3) — audit the code, fork it, or run it yourself. Maptionnaire is explicitly "not an open-source service" (Kyttä et al., 2023)
  • EU data residency — hosted in Frankfurt, Germany; self-host option for full sovereignty
  • Standard data export — GeoJSON and CSV out of the box, importable in QGIS, ArcGIS, R, or Python without conversion

The price gap

"Maptionnaire is not an open-source service. […] In the Maptionnaire service there are three different product levels […]. Prices vary according to these alternatives, ranging from $950 USD per month for a single project to $50 000 USD per year for enterprise solutions." — Kyttä et al. (2023), Chapter 4 of Burnett (ed.), Evaluating Participatory Mapping Software, Springer

Mapsurvey is free on mapsurvey.org and free to self-host under AGPLv3. No per-survey, per-response, or per-seat charges. If your project budget has room for Maptionnaire, the gap is real money — funding you can redirect to participation outreach, design work, or analysis time.
