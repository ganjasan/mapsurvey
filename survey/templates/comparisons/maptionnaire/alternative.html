Mapsurvey vs Maptionnaire

A free, open-source Maptionnaire alternative

Mapsurvey is a participatory mapping platform with no per-survey pricing, full data export, and optional self-hosting. Same core job as Maptionnaire — different economics.

Why teams choose Mapsurvey

  • Free to start — no per-survey fees, no seat licensing, no tier that gates your first real project
  • Open source (AGPLv3) — audit the code, fork it, or run it yourself. No vendor lock-in
  • EU data residency — hosted in Frankfurt, Germany; self-host option for full sovereignty
  • Standard data export — GeoJSON and CSV out of the box, importable in QGIS, ArcGIS, R or Python without conversion

Pricing

Maptionnaire offers three subscription tiers — Point, Collect, and Communicate — with pricing based on the number of surveys you publish rather than seats or responses. Specific prices are not listed publicly; enterprise customers receive a custom quote.

Mapsurvey is free for hosted use on mapsurvey.org, and free to run yourself under AGPLv3. We do not charge per survey, per response, or per team member.
