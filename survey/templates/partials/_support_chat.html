Included by base_landing.html, base.html and editor/editor_base.html. The creator bases pass `editor_bubble=True` (change onboarding-friction, #638): the bubble shows on a desktop and never on a phone, carries a "×" that hides it in this browser (localStorage['msSupportBubble']), and the "?" menu's "Show chat button" (`.support-bubble-restore`, window.showSupportBubble) brings it back. On the editor the widget never speaks first, bubble or not: no unread pop-up, no pop-out button; the `.support-unread` dot on the "?" / "Help & support" entry points (window.openSupportChat) is its signal. `hide_bubble=True` hides the bubble outright.
