Sites is a new item in the sidebar. Build a public site for a project from blocks — text, FAQ, a timeline of the phases, pictures, video, documents to download, a map of the project area — and add cards for your surveys: each card follows its survey from "coming soon" to "open" to "closed, see the results". Show what residents said with charts and maps from the results, and announce an open house with registration and a calendar invitation. One survey can appear on any number of sites.

Why. An engagement is longer than one survey: a pilot, the main round, feedback on the proposal, events. Flyers and QR codes need one address that stays the same from the first pilot to the final plan, and until now that page had to be built by hand on another site. Sites are part of Pro; a published site stays online even if the plan ends.
