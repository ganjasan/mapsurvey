--- title: Put your survey on your own site kind: new link: editor ---

Share now has an Embed on your site section: copy one line of code and the survey runs inside your page, on a city website, a project page or a client portal. Tilda, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace and WordPress all take it; in Notion you just paste the survey link. Responses land here as usual, and Responses → Traffic Sources shows which site they came from.

Forms grow with their content, so no inner scrollbars. For a map, set the height you want in the code. If you only want the survey on your own site, list its address under Sites allowed to embed; leave the list empty for Notion and other platforms that embed from a link.

Why. Creators kept asking to keep respondents on their own pages instead of sending them to a link, and it works everywhere now, including Safari on iPhone.
