--- title: Edit a live survey without starting over kind: new link: editor ---

Fixing wording, help text, translations or map settings, or adding an answer option, on a published survey now saves straight to the live survey. There is no draft to create first and no new version.

Adding, removing or reordering questions and sections still waits. Click any of those controls and choose Start unpublished changes. Respondents keep the current survey while you edit. When you are done, click Publish changes.

Why versions exist. Publishing structural changes creates a new version of the survey, so every answer stays tied to the questions that respondent actually saw. Without versions, deleting a question would delete the answers people already gave to it. Changing a question's type or removing an option would leave one column holding answers to two different questions. Responses shows every version together under All versions, and you can still filter and export each version on its own.

Why this change. Many of you opened a published survey to fix a typo and found every field locked. Now only the edits that could break answers you have already collected are locked.
