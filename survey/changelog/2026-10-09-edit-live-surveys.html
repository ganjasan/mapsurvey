--- title: Edit a live survey without starting over kind: new link: editor ---

Wording, help text, translations, answer options and map settings on a published survey now save straight to the live survey. There is no draft to create first and no new version.

To add, remove or reorder questions and sections, click any of those controls and choose Start unpublished changes. You can edit as much as you like while respondents keep the current survey. When you are done, click Publish changes.

Why. A lot of you opened a published survey to fix a typo and found every field locked. The lock now covers only the edits that could break answers you have already collected.
