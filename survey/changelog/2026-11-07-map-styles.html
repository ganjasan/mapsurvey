Map styles is a new page in the sidebar, under Library. It holds the five base maps — Streets, Satellite, Topo and two made for printing, Paper A and Paper B — and the ones you make: duplicate a preset, open the editor, change what stays on the map, the colours of land, parks and water, the look of streets and labels, and see the result at once, online and as the printed form will render it. Pick any style in a survey's settings; export it as a file and import it into another workspace.

Why. Planners and consultancies want the survey map to match their own cartography and to stay the same across every survey, and a paper form needs a map that survives a photocopy. Until now that meant asking us for a custom base map. Creating and editing styles is part of Pro; using any style that exists never is, so a survey keeps its map whatever the plan.
