--- title: Coverage surface for drawn areas and routes kind: new link: editor image: img/changelog/2026-10-06-coverage-surface.png ---

On the Responses map, a polygon or line question now has a Coverage action in its layer menu. It draws, for every spot on the map, how many respondents included it in their area or passed within a set distance of it on their route, so where people agree stands out instead of hiding under a stack of translucent shapes.

The row states the count ("≤ 7 of 12 respondents"); the settings let you change the cell size and, for routes, the corridor width. The surface follows the table selection like a heatmap does.

Why. "Draw the area you consider the centre" and "draw your route" are asked to find agreement, and the map could show it for points and spray but not for shapes.
