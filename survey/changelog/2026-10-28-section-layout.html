--- title: Put the questions on the right, make them wider, or next to a picture kind: new link: editor ---

Each section now has a Layout block. Choose what fills the screen beside the questions — the Map, a Picture or nothing (Form) — which side the questions sit on, and how wide they are: the usual narrow column, a third or half of the screen.

A welcome page with a photo of the place on one half and the introduction with a Start button on the other is now three settings: Picture, Right, Half the screen. A Picture section uses its own picture, or the survey's cover image when it has none.

Side and width apply on computer screens; on a phone the questions keep covering the map, and a picture becomes a band at the top of the page. Existing surveys look exactly as before.
