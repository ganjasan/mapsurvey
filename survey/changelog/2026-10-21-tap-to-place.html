--- title: A tap on the map can place the mark kind: new link: editor ---

Point questions have a new setting: A tap on the map places a mark. Turn it on, and respondents can tap the map to drop a mark straight away instead of pressing the question's button first. The mark opens the same pop-up and counts towards the same limits. The button still works.

The setting only works while the point question is the only map question on its page. If another one is on the page, the question's "tap to place" badge turns grey and taps do nothing.

Why. Respondents often click the map first and nothing happens. Point questions are the questions people skip most.
