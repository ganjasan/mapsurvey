--- title: Paper forms come back in from photos kind: new ---

Print numbered copies of your paper form: every copy carries its own number on every page. After the event, photograph or scan the filled forms in any order and drop them on Responses → Enter a response → From photos of paper forms. Each page finds its copy by itself, is read for you — ticks, numbers, handwriting — and every form opens as a prefilled response with the photo beside it: check page by page, correct, press Finish. A page you missed can be photographed right there. Dots, lines and areas drawn on the printed map land on the real map where the respondent drew them, with what they wrote about each mark.

Why. Stacks of forms after an open house were typed in by hand, or never. Reading them takes a phone and a few minutes; checking takes the same care as before. Pro.
