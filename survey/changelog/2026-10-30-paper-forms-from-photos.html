--- title: Paper forms come back in from photos kind: new ---

Photograph or scan the filled sheets of a printed paper form and drop the files on Responses → Enter a response → From photos. Each page is read for you — ticks, numbers, handwriting — and every sheet opens as a prefilled response with the photo beside it: check, correct, press Finish. Fields the reader was unsure about are marked.

Why. Stacks of sheets after an open house were typed in by hand, or never. Reading them takes a phone and a few minutes; checking takes the same care as before. Marks on the printed map are the next step. Pro.
