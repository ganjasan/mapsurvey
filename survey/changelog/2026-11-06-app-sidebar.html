--- title: A sidebar for your workspace kind: new link: editor ---

Every creator page now has a sidebar on the left. Your workspace is at the top: click its name to switch to another one. Below it are Surveys, API (your tokens), MCP (connecting Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants), and your workspace's Members, Plan & billing and General, one click away instead of inside a menu.

Inside a survey the sidebar shrinks to icons so the map keeps its width. On the survey list you can collapse it too, and it stays the way you left it. On a phone it opens from the ☰ button.
