--- title: Three ways to start a survey kind: new link: editor_survey_start ---

+ New Survey now asks how you want to begin: start from scratch (one click, straight into the editor, the map already on your city), draft with AI (one sentence in, a full draft out), or — soon — connect your own AI agent through MCP. Tick "Always start this way" and the question never comes back; "Start another way…" in the dashboard's ⋯ menu brings it back when you want it.

Why. The create page opened on Berlin for everyone and put the AI brief first, whatever you had in mind. Now the map opens where you are when your browser allows it, or where your workspace's last survey was — and the way you like to start is yours to keep.
