--- title: Translate a whole survey in one table kind: new link: editor ---

Every survey now has a Translations tab, between Survey and Responses. It lists every text of the survey once — page titles, questions, help texts, answer options, the Next button, the thank-you page — in the order respondents see it, with the original on the left and the translation on the right. "Other" and "(Select all that apply)" appear once and one translation fills every place they are used; a row can be translated separately when the same words need different wording in context. Each row links back to its question in the editor.

Rows show what is missing and what is outdated — a translation whose original was edited after it was written — and the publish warning counts both. Export the table as a CSV for a translator, import it back, and the report says what was filled, skipped or not found. Languages are added right there, and a second language of the survey can serve as the source column for a translator who does not read the primary one.

The translation fields in the question, section and group dialogs now show the stored text and open its row on the new tab, so there is one place where translations are written.
