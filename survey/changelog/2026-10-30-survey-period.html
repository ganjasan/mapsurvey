--- title: Open and close a survey on the dates you set kind: new link: editor ---

Settings → Survey period takes an opening time, a closing time, or both. Before the start respondents see a page saying when the survey opens; at the end it closes by itself, within five minutes, exactly as if you had pressed Close — and the status chip shows what comes next.

Reopening a survey by hand clears a closing time that has passed, so it stays open until you set a new one. The same fields are available over the REST API and the MCP server.
