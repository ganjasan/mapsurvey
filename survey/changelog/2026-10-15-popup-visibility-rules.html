--- title: Pop-up sub-questions can depend on an earlier choice in the same pop-up kind: new ---

A sub-question of a map question (or of an "Objects on the map" question) now has the same Visibility block as a regular question. Pick an earlier choice sub-question of the same pop-up and the options that should reveal it, and the follow-up appears only for marks where the respondent picked one of them.

One point question with a "Type of place" choice and a follow-up per type now replaces a separate map question per type with the shared follow-ups copied by hand. The rule is decided for every mark separately, a hidden follow-up is never required and never stored, and the section list and the question modal show the same branch badges you know from conditional questions.
