--- title: We will tell you here when something changes kind: new link: whats_new ---

From now on, a card like this one appears once in the editor after an update: what changed, and why. Older updates stay on the What's new page, under the gift icon in the top bar or in the account menu.

"Got it" or the × closes the card. "Don't show these" switches cards off; the page stays, and you can switch them back on there.
