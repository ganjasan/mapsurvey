--- title: Budget questions — split a budget, fund projects, place priced items on the map kind: new link: editor ---

A new question type, Budget: respondents share a fixed budget between your priorities, or tick projects until the money runs out. And a budget on a question group: give the map questions inside it a price — a bench £2k each, a path £50 a metre, a flower bed £30 a square metre — and respondents place marks until the group's total is spent. A bar shows what is left; Next waits until the rule you set is met.

Why. Budget and precept consultations were being built from ranking questions, which say what matters but not what people would give up. Responses, exports and public results now show the average share per item, the projects that fit the budget together, and where the placed items went.
