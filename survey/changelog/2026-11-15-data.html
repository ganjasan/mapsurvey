--- title: Data: your workspace's datasets in one place, shown wherever you need them kind: new link: editor_data ---

Data is a new page in the sidebar. It lists every place dataset of your workspace — the layers you uploaded or drew, the answers your surveys collect — and takes new files: GeoPackage, zipped shapefile, KML, GeoJSON or a CSV with coordinates. Give a dataset a licence and a maintainer, label its fields and keep personal columns private. Show it in several surveys and on a site at once: it is linked, not copied, so a new version (matched row by row on its key, answers kept) shows everywhere. Save views — "trees in poor condition", "answers as counts" — group an engagement with tags, and work with all of it from the API and MCP.

Why. Councils and consultancies kept uploading the same register to every survey, and nobody could see which surveys showed a dataset before changing it. Public maps on results pages and sites now always show grids of counts, never a single answer.
