--- title: Coverage you can read, and a contrast control kind: fixed link: editor ---

The Coverage surface on the Responses map now counts people, not shapes: someone who drew two overlapping areas counts once, and the row's "of N respondents" is the number of people.

The strongest spot on the map is now full colour, so agreement shows up even when nowhere near everyone drew the same place. In the coverage settings, Contrast decides how quickly the colour builds up and Min. people hides spots fewer people chose. A small chart under the sliders shows the result for every count.

Why. On real surveys the surface was so pale it was hard to see at all.
