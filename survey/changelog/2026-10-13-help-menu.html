--- title: Help is one click away kind: new ---

A ? in the editor header opens a small help menu: what's new, the FAQ, and "Help & support" — a chat with us. If you leave the page before we answer, the reply reaches you by email, and the conversation continues there.

Why. Support used to be hidden in the account menu, and a question asked while we were away was lost: the chat did not know who you were. Now it does — you are signed in, so there is nothing to fill in.
