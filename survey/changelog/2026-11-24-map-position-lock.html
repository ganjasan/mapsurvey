--- title: The start map no longer moves when you scroll past it kind: fixed link: editor ---

The Default map position map in survey settings now opens locked. Scrolling the page with the pointer or a finger over it scrolls the page and nothing else. Press Change position on the map to move it; it locks again when you press Done, click elsewhere or scroll on. Place search still moves the map directly.

Why. Every move of that map is saved at once, so a scroll through the settings could zoom it and quietly replace a carefully framed start position.
