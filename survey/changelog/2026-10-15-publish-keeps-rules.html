--- title: Publishing changes keeps your conditional logic, form sections and button labels kind: fixed ---

Publishing Unpublished changes on a live survey no longer resets what you did not touch: conditional questions and sections keep their Visibility rules, form sections stay forms, custom button labels (and their translations) and per-section hidden layers survive the publish.

Why. The working copy behind "Unpublished changes" was created with a list of fields that predated these settings, so every publish quietly put them back to their defaults. If a survey of yours lost its rules or labels on a publish, the previous version still has them: write to us and we restore them, or restore the version yourself and publish it again.
