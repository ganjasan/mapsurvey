--- title: Question hints show up properly again, including on the map kind: fixed link: editor ---

The hint you write under a question now appears once, small and grey under the question, for every question type. On map questions it used to appear twice, the second copy larger than the question itself.

Sub-questions that open on the map, when a respondent places a point or taps an object, now show their hints too, and ratings there use the style you picked (scale, labeled list or stars) instead of plain radio buttons.

Why. Hints became formatted text, and the old layout could not hold it. In map pop-ups hints were never shown at all.
