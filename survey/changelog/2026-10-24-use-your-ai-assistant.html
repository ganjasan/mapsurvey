--- title: Start a survey from Claude, ChatGPT or your own assistant kind: new link: editor_survey_start_agent ---

The third card on New survey is live: Use your AI assistant. Pick yours — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Le Chat, Perplexity, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code and more — and follow the shortest way it allows. For Claude it is one button: the connector form opens filled in, you press Add, Connect and Allow. The page notices by itself when you are connected.

Then write what you want to find out, open the prompt in your assistant, and the draft appears on your dashboard. If your assistant cannot connect to outside tools yet, the page says so and offers the Survey wizard instead. Building surveys through an assistant is part of Pro.
