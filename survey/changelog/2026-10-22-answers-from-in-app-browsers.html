--- title: Answers from the Facebook and Instagram apps save again kind: fixed ---

Between 13 and 22 October, people who opened a survey from a link inside the Facebook or Instagram app on an iPhone could not send their answers: every "Next" came back with a security error. The same change made a few editor actions fail for creators — moving responses to the trash, publishing public results, dismissing these cards. Both are fixed; nothing you saved was lost, but answers those respondents tried to give in that window never reached you.

Why. Surveys embedded on other sites got cookies that work inside a frame, and those cookies were sent to everyone. In-app browsers on iOS refuse them. Embedded surveys now keep their session another way, and everyone else gets ordinary cookies.
