--- title: A loading bar, and buttons that show they are working kind: new ---

Creator pages now show a thin bar at the top while the next page or a save is on its way, and a button you pressed turns busy at once: a spinner, and no second send if you press it again.

Why. On a slow connection the Responses page can take a few seconds to arrive, and nothing on screen said the click had registered. People clicked again, and some forms were sent several times in a row.
