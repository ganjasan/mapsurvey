--- title: Lay out each page your way — picture, side, width, a quiet column kind: new link: editor image: img/changelog/2026-10-29-survey-layout-options.png ---

Each section now has a Layout block. Put a picture where the map would be (a welcome page with a photo of the place), move the questions to the right, make them a third or half of the screen, and set them as a calm column in the middle of the panel.

On a map page you can keep the questions open while respondents place a point or draw: the crosshair and the drawing buttons aim at the map beside them, so nobody loses the question they are answering.

Every setting is optional and applies on computer screens; phones keep the familiar layout, and existing surveys look exactly as before.
