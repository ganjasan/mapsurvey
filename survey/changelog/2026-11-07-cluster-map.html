--- title: Clusters for point answers kind: new ---

Points that sit close together can now merge into one circle with a count. The circle splits into smaller groups and single points as you zoom in or click it. It is easier to read than plain markers when answers are spread far apart, for example research sites around the world.

On Responses → Map, open a point layer's ⋮ menu and choose Create Clusters. The clusters follow your filter, and removing them brings the points back. On a public results page, set a map block's Visualization to Clusters.
