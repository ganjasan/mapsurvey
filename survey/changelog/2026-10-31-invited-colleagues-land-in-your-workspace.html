--- title: Invited colleagues now land in your workspace kind: fixed link: editor ---

Someone you invite from Workspace → Members now arrives where you invited them. A colleague who creates an account from the invitation link joins your workspace straight away, with no extra workspace of their own, and sees your dashboard with a note that they have joined. Someone who confirms an invited address later lands there too, instead of on a page asking them to start a survey.

An editor who does not yet see any surveys reads why: surveys they create, and the ones you add them to, appear on their dashboard.
