--- title: Responses no longer label answers as problems kind: fixed link: editor ---

The Issues column is gone from Responses. Most of its marks were wrong: "Duplicate" caught different people on the same kind of phone, and "Incomplete" caught people who skipped an optional last page. Some of you deleted real answers because of them. If you did, open Trash on Responses: deleted responses can be restored from there.

Completed now means "answered every required question". Reaching the last page no longer matters, so completion rates on the Overview, the dashboard and in exports change, usually up. A small grey question mark marks responses that left required questions unanswered; point at it to see which.

Min and Max on number questions now apply. Respondents can't enter a number outside them.
