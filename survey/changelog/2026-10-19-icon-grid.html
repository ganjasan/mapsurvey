--- title: Show options as an icon grid kind: new ---

Single and multiple choice questions have a new Display as option: Icon grid. Every option becomes a tile with an icon from the map icon set above its label. Options that share a group sit together under one heading. On a single choice question, the grid folds to the chosen row once someone picks, and "Show all options" opens it again.

Why. A list of fifteen kinds of place takes two screens to scroll in a pop-up on a phone. As tiles, the whole list fits, and people find their option by its picture.
