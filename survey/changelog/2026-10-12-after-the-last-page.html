--- title: "After the last page" replaces "Redirect URL" kind: fixed link: editor ---

In Survey settings you now choose what respondents see when they finish: the thank-you page, or a web address you give, such as your project's Facebook page. The address has to be a full link starting with https://, and the field tells you if it is not.

Why. The old "Redirect URL" box accepted anything. A hashtag or a short message typed there kept respondents on the last page, pressing Finish again and again. Surveys that had such a value now show the thank-you page, and a link typed after a "#" works again.
