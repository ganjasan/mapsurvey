--- title: Question groups kind: new link: editor ---

Put several questions under one title and description, and show or hide them all with one visibility rule. Click Add group next to New Question, or next to Add Sub-question to group the questions of a map pop-up, then drag questions into it. A group shows as a framed card, a plain heading, or a block that opens on tap.

Why. When twenty questions depended on one answer, the same rule had to be set twenty times, and respondents saw one long list. A group sets it once — for example "Your visit" appears in the pop-up only after the type of place is picked.
