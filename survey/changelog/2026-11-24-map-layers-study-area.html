--- title: A study area for your survey, and layers that show on every map kind: new link: editor ---

Study area. Type the place your survey is about — a neighbourhood, a park, a street — and pick it from OpenStreetMap; a street becomes a corridor of the width you choose. Adjust the outline by its corners. The respondent map opens on it, the rest of the map is greyed out, and you can refuse map answers outside it with a message of your own. The printed form, the Responses map and Reports maps follow it.

Layers on every map. A layer you add goes on every section with a map — including sections you add later — switched on. Each section's Map card in Build shows what its map holds; tick a layer off where it does not belong. You no longer need an "Objects on the map" question just to show a layer: that question is for asking about its objects. Uploads take GeoPackage, zipped shapefiles, KML and CSV too, in any coordinate system, and every layer is now a dataset in Data, so one upload can serve several surveys.
