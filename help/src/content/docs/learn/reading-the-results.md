- Agreement is the finding: count respondents per place, not pins per place (one person can place three). Pszów: 26 of 52 on one platform; Remington: 20 pins from 17 people at one intersection.
- Clean first: pins in water, polygons covering the whole town, test sessions, empty sessions (Alderton et al. 2026 on self-reported geometry).
- Heat maps show concentration but hide counts; use them to look, use grids or clusters to report. Watch for the "most engaged respondent" dominating a layer.
- Read the attributes with the geometry: the sub-question columns are what make a cluster interpretable.
- Join to what you already have (existing bins, boundaries, land use) in QGIS or R via the GeoJSON/CSV export.
- Triangulate: a problem that appears in the pins, the popups and the open text (Remington's bridge: 39 pins, 40 comments, 32 unprompted mentions).
- When you build it: `/how-to/export-responses/`, `/reference/exports/`.
