- Results that vanish kill the next round: participants disengage when their role is purely informational (Brown & Kyttä 2014; Kahila-Tani et al. 2019 on the weak link between map data and the plan).
- Say on page one what will happen with the answers, then do it: Whitehouse recommended six bins within a month; Pszów fed the data into design workshops.
- What to show: aggregates and agreement, not individuals. No free-text answers in public; small groups masked; pins without the sub-question text unless chosen deliberately.
- Formats: a public results page, a short written summary with the three main findings, a map image for the council paper.
- Report the sample honestly: how many, which channels, who is missing.
- When you build it: `/how-to/share-results-publicly/`.
