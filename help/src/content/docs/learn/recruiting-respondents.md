- Sampling, not software, decides whether the data can inform a decision (Brown & Kyttä 2014; Brown, Kelly & Whitall 2014 on volunteer vs random samples). Internet PPGIS with random household sampling averages 13–15% response.
- Channels and what each reaches: QR flyers where people already are (Remington), council website and Facebook (Whitehouse: 27 responses in two days, ten more on a reshare), the town hall's site (Pszów: ~100 on day one), posters for year-round reporting (Olney).
- The digital divide: paper copies and in-person sessions as part of the design, not a fallback.
- Count completions, not link opens (350 opens → 146 completed); track links per channel so the second push goes where the first worked.
- Timing: most responses land in the first 48 hours; a second share a week later; keep the survey open for the slow channels.
- When you build it: link to the sharing how-to once it exists; `/how-to/share-results-publicly/` for closing the loop.
