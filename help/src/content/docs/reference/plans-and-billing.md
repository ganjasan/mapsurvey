Plans are per organisation (your workspace). The organisation owner sees them under **Plan & billing** in the account menu and on the **Organization Settings** page.

## Free and Pro

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Surveys, responses, members | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Question types, map questions, reference layers, exports, public results, embedding, translations | All | All |
| **Remove "Made with Mapsurvey" from your surveys** | — | Yes: the **Show the "Made with Mapsurvey" badge** switch in survey settings becomes editable |
| **Consulting, analysis and reports from the Mapsurvey team** | — | Yes: the **Pro help** request form in the account menu |

Everything that is free today stays free. Pro is a service plan: direct access to the Mapsurvey team for project consulting, analysis of your responses and reports for councils, clients and funders, plus a clean, unbranded survey.

A Free organisation that opens a Pro-only page sees "This is a Pro feature" with a link to the plans.

## Price

$49 a month, or $490 a year, per organisation. Prices are in USD (EUR at the same figure); other currencies are converted at checkout.

## How payment works

PayPro Global is the merchant of record: checkout, cards, tax, invoices, refunds and chargebacks are handled by them, not by Mapsurvey.

| | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Starts with | **Upgrade — monthly** | **Upgrade — yearly** or **Get a quote** |
| Payment methods | Credit or debit card, PayPal | Card, PayPal, bank transfer or purchase order. **Get a quote** opens the same checkout in quote mode and produces a PDF you can put through procurement |
| Renewal | Automatic each month | Automatic each year, or — after a bank transfer or purchase order — by invoice: PayPro Global emails a payment link before the renewal date |

Only the organisation owner can start a checkout; other members see "Only the organisation owner can upgrade."

After checkout, PayPro Global returns you to a Mapsurvey page that waits for the payment confirmation — usually a few seconds. Pro is active when the billing page shows **Pro** with the **Active** badge. An order paid by bank transfer shows "awaiting your bank transfer" until the money arrives, typically within 2–5 business days, and you get an email when it does.

## Managing a subscription

On **Plan & billing**:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Update payment method** | Opens PayPro Global to change the card |
| **Manage in PayPro customer account** | Opens your PayPro Global account: invoices, receipts, subscription |
| **Last invoice** | The latest invoice from PayPro Global |
| **Switch to yearly** / **Switch to monthly** | Changes the billing period; the change takes effect at the next charge |
| **Cancel plan** | Asks why, then **Confirm cancellation** |
| **Resubscribe** | Restarts a cancelled or lapsed subscription |
| **Email me a PayPro login link** | Sends a sign-in link for the PayPro Global customer account |

The switch, cancel and resubscribe controls are not yet active on mapsurvey.org: they appear once Mapsurvey's connection to PayPro Global's API is switched on. Until then, cancel or change the plan from the PayPro customer account (**Email me a PayPro login link**) or write to support.

## States you may see

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Pro, renewing on the date shown |
| **Payment failed** | The last charge failed; Pro stays active until the period end while PayPro Global retries |
| **Cancelling** | Cancelled; Pro stays active until the date shown, then the organisation returns to Free |
| **Ended** | The Pro plan has ended. Everything you built is still there; only the Pro extras are off |
| **Change requested…** | A switch or cancellation is waiting for PayPro Global's confirmation |

## Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time. The organisation keeps Pro until the end of the period already paid for, then returns to Free — every survey, response and setting stays where it is; only the Pro extras switch off. If a payment is refunded or charged back, the organisation returns to Free immediately. Refund requests go to PayPro Global.
