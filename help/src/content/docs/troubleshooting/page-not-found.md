Someone opened a survey link and saw **This survey isn’t available**, with the note that it may not be published yet, or may have been closed or removed. Two neighbouring pages belong here too: **This survey is no longer accepting responses.** and **This survey requires a password to access.**

## Why it happens

A survey link only works while the survey is open to its audience. The page does not say which condition applied, by design; the editor toolbar does.

- **Draft.** Not published yet. The toolbar reads **Draft — not collecting responses**.
- **Testing.** The toolbar reads **Testing — invite-link access**. Only the test link opens the survey.
- **Being checked.** After publishing, a survey can be held while its content is checked. The editor shows **Your survey is being reviewed.** Respondents see the unavailable page until the check completes, usually within a day, and you receive an email when it does.
- **Closed or archived.** Respondents see the survey's name with **This survey is no longer accepting responses.** The toolbar reads **Closed**.
- **Password.** Respondents must enter the password you set under **Password & Test Access**.
- **Removed, or a broken link.** The survey was deleted, or the link was retyped or cut short.

Signed-in editors and owners bypass these checks, so the link can work for you and not for the person you sent it to.

## What to do

1. Open the survey in the editor and read the status in the toolbar.
2. If it says **Draft**, click **🌐 Publish**. For a dry run first, use **🔗 Share test link** and send that link instead of the public one.
3. If the survey is being reviewed, wait for the email. The hold lifts on its own; you do not need to republish.
4. If it says **Closed**, open the publishing panel and click **Reopen survey** under **Collection**.
5. If the survey has a password, send it with the link, or remove it under **Password & Test Access › Remove Password**.
6. Send the link again, copied from the share page, and open it yourself in a private browser window to see what respondents see.

## If it keeps happening

[Contact support](https://mapsurvey.org/?support=1) with the survey link, the status in the editor toolbar and the page the respondent saw. If the survey is being reviewed and more than a day has passed, include the date you published.
