A public engagement is longer than one survey: a pilot, the main survey, a round of feedback on the proposal, open-house events. A **landing page** is the one address residents need for all of it. Put it on flyers, posters and QR codes; it stays the same while the surveys behind it open and close.

Landing pages are part of **Pro**. A workspace on the Pro trial can create one.

## Create a page

1. Open **Landing pages** in the sidebar.
2. Type a title, for example *Riverside Park plan*, and press **New page**. The page opens in the builder as a draft.
3. Under **Page**, add a summary of one or two sentences and a cover picture. The summary is also what people see when the link is shared.

## Add blocks

Press **Add block** and choose what to add:

- **Text** — formatted text: what the project is, why residents are asked, what happens with their answers.
- **FAQ** — questions and answers that open on a tap.
- **Timeline** — the phases of the engagement with their dates. Tick **Now** on the phase you are in; earlier phases show as done.
- **Image** — a picture with a description for screen readers and an optional caption.
- **Video** — a YouTube or Vimeo link.
- **Survey card** — one of your surveys. The card follows the survey: *Open* with a **Take part** button, *Opens on …* before its period starts, *Coming soon* while it is a draft, and *Closed* with a link to its [public results](/how-to/share-results-publicly/) once you publish them.
- **Button** — a link to anything else: the draft plan, the council's page, an event sign-up.
- **Documents** — files to download: the input summary, the open-house boards, the draft plan. PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, CSV or plain text, up to 25 MB each and 20 per block. Each file gets a title (the file name to start with) that you can change; tick **Remove** to take one off.
- **Results** — what residents said in a round: a chart of a choice, rating or number question, or a map (markers, heatmap or clusters) of a map question, from any survey of your workspace. Answers are counted as on the survey's [public results page](/how-to/share-results-publicly/): only reviewed, non-deleted responses, small groups masked, written answers never shown. The block updates by itself as answers come in.
- **Map** — the project area on a map. Upload a GeoJSON file with the boundary, a route or a few points (up to 1 MB; export it from QGIS or draw it on [geojson.io](https://geojson.io)), pick a colour and one of your [map styles](/how-to/map-styles/). A shape's `name` shows when someone points at it.

Give a block a **heading** and it also appears in the menu at the top of the page. Drag blocks by the handle to change their order.

## Events and registration

The **Event** block announces an open house, a walk or a workshop: date, start and end time in the event's time zone, the place (with an optional map link), a few words about it, and an optional number of seats.

While **Registration open** is ticked and the event has not ended, the block carries a short form: name, email and a box the resident ticks to agree. They get an email with the details, a calendar file and a link to cancel; a cancelled registration frees its seat. The same email registers only once.

Under the event in the builder, **Attendee list** shows who registered, with a CSV download and **Remove** / **Remove all**. Names and emails are for this event only: Mapsurvey deletes them automatically **30 days after the event** — the form says so to every resident. Owners, admins and editors of the workspace see the list; viewers do not.

## Surveys on the page

**Surveys on this page** lists the surveys the page shows. Add one there or straight from a survey card. A survey can be on any number of landing pages — the same city-wide survey on two neighbourhood pages, for example. Removing a survey from the page removes its cards.

## Publish

Press **Publish**. The page is at `mapsurvey.org/p/<address>/`; change the address under **Page** before you print it — changing it later breaks the links already out there. **Share** has the link and a QR code to download.

- **Public** pages may be listed by search engines.
- **Unlisted** pages are reachable only by people who have the link.

Until you publish, only members of your workspace can open the page; they see a *Draft* banner. **Unpublish** takes the page offline at any time.

## If your Pro plan ends

A published page stays online, so the QR codes you printed keep working. You can still unpublish or delete it, but not edit it, until the workspace is on Pro again.
