You end up with your organisation on Pro: the "Made with Mapsurvey" badge can be switched off on every survey, and the **Pro help** request form is open to your team.

## Before you start

- You are the **owner** of the organisation. Admins see the billing page but cannot start a checkout; other members are told to ask the owner.
- Have a card or PayPal account ready. For the yearly plan, a bank transfer or purchase order also works — see step 2.

## Steps

1. Open the account menu and choose **Plan & billing**. The page shows the current plan (**Free**) and the Pro offer: **Consulting, analysis and reports from the Mapsurvey team**, **Remove "Made with Mapsurvey" from your surveys**, and everything in Free, which stays free.
   <!-- screenshot: the Plan & billing page with the Upgrade buttons -->
2. Choose how to pay:
   - **Upgrade — monthly** ($49 a month) or **Upgrade — yearly** ($490 a year) opens the PayPro Global checkout for card or PayPal. The yearly checkout also offers bank transfer and purchase order.
   - **Get a quote** opens the yearly checkout in quote mode and produces a PDF you can put through procurement; pay it later by any of the methods above.
3. Complete the checkout on PayPro Global. Your email and the organisation are filled in already; PayPro Global handles the card, tax and the invoice.
4. Wait on the "Thank you" page Mapsurvey shows afterwards. It reads **Activating your Pro plan…** and updates on its own, usually within seconds, to **Your Pro plan is active.** If you paid by bank transfer, it says the plan activates when the transfer arrives (typically 2–5 business days) and you get an email then.
5. Go back to **Plan & billing** to confirm the **Pro** plan shows the **Active** badge, the renewal date and **Last invoice**.

## Result

Every survey's **Settings** now lets you switch off **Show the "Made with Mapsurvey" badge** — on the survey, the thanks page and the public results page. **Pro help** in the account menu opens the request form for consulting, analysis or a report. Everything else is unchanged.

To change your mind later: **Switch to yearly** / **Switch to monthly** takes effect at the next charge; **Cancel plan** keeps Pro until the end of the paid period, then returns the organisation to Free with every survey, response and setting in place. Invoices, receipts and refunds are in **Manage in PayPro customer account**.
