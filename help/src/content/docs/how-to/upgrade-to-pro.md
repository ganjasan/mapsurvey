You end up with your organisation on Pro: the "Made with Mapsurvey" badge can be switched off on every survey, and the **Advice on your survey and results** request form is open to your team.

## Before you start

- You are the **owner** of the organisation. Admins see the billing page but cannot start a checkout; other members are told to ask the owner.
- Have a card or PayPal account ready. For the yearly plan, a bank transfer or purchase order also works — see step 2.

## Try Pro first

Every Free organisation gets a free **Pro trial** before paying. It is a number of uses of each Pro extra, not a number of days of Pro. Controls that are part of Pro carry a violet **Pro** mark; while the trial can still start it reads **Pro · free to try**.

| Pro extra | Included in the trial |
|---|---|
| Calls to the REST API and the MCP server | 500 |
| Surveys without the "Made with Mapsurvey" badge | 1 |
| Requests for **Advice on your survey and results**: a check of your survey before launch, how to reach respondents, how to read what came in | 1 |
| Paper responses typed in with **Enter a response** on Responses | No limit |

What is left can be used for 90 days from the day the trial starts. No card is needed.

The trial starts by itself the first time anyone in the organisation uses a Pro extra — switches the badge off, enters a paper response, sends a request to the team, or calls the API. The owner gets an e-mail saying who started it and with what. The owner can also start it in advance: open **Plan & billing** from the account menu and press **Try Pro**.

The **Pro trial** block on Plan & billing shows how much of each extra is used and the day the trial ends. A week before the end the owner gets one more e-mail listing what stops.

Things to know:

- The trial belongs to the organisation's owner: each owner gets one trial, in one organisation. A second organisation you own can buy Pro, but it cannot start another trial.
- A use the trial counted is not given back. Switching the badge on again does not return the survey without a badge.
- When the trial ends, or an extra is used up, the organisation is on Free for that extra again. The badge comes back on a survey you switched it off for, entering paper responses closes, and API calls answer `pro_required`. Nothing you built is deleted, including the responses entered from paper.
- If you buy Pro during the trial, nothing is counted while Pro lasts.

## Steps

1. Open the account menu and choose **Plan & billing**. The page shows the current plan (**Free**) and the Pro offer: **Advice on your survey and results**, **Remove "Made with Mapsurvey" from your surveys**, and everything in Free, which stays free.
   <!-- screenshot: the Plan & billing page with the Upgrade buttons -->
2. Choose how to pay:
   - **Upgrade — monthly** ($49 a month) or **Upgrade — yearly** ($490 a year) opens the PayPro Global checkout for card or PayPal. The yearly checkout also offers bank transfer and purchase order.
   - **Get a quote** opens the yearly checkout in quote mode and produces a PDF you can put through procurement; pay it later by any of the methods above.
3. Complete the checkout on PayPro Global. Your email and the organisation are filled in already; PayPro Global handles the card, tax and the invoice.
4. Wait on the "Thank you" page Mapsurvey shows afterwards. It reads **Activating your Pro plan…** and updates on its own, usually within seconds, to **Your Pro plan is active.** If you paid by bank transfer, it says the plan activates when the transfer arrives (typically 2–5 business days) and you get an email then.
5. Go back to **Plan & billing** to confirm the **Pro** plan shows the **Active** badge, the renewal date and **Last invoice**.

## Result

Every survey's **Settings** now lets you switch off **Show the "Made with Mapsurvey" badge** — on the survey, the thanks page and the public results page. **Advice on your survey and results** in the account menu and the "?" menu opens the request form. Hands-on work — building a survey for you, an analysis kit, a written report — is quoted separately. Everything else is unchanged.

To change your mind later: **Switch to yearly** / **Switch to monthly** takes effect at the next charge; **Cancel plan** keeps Pro until the end of the paid period, then returns the organisation to Free with every survey, response and setting in place. Invoices, receipts and refunds are in **Manage in PayPro customer account**.
