You end up with a printable PDF of your survey: every question with boxes and lines, the map of each page with your reference layers, and a code on every sheet so the answers can be matched back to this survey.

## Before you start

- You have the **editor** role or higher on the survey.
- The survey has the questions and pages you want on paper. The form is built from the survey as it is when you download it, so finish editing first.
- For a map page, set the page's start position and zoom on **Build → page settings → map**. The printed map shows exactly that view.

## Steps

1. Open the survey's **Share** page and find **Print a paper form** at the bottom.
2. Pick the **language** (one form per language) and the **paper size**, A4 or US Letter.
3. Choose how many to print:
   - **Numbered copies** (up to 200 per download). Every copy carries its own number on every page, so photos and scans of the filled forms [sort themselves](/how-to/enter-paper-forms-from-photos/). Numbers never repeat: the next download continues where the last one stopped, in any language or paper size.
   - **One copy to photocopy**. One form without a number. Every photocopy is the same, so when you bring them back from photos you upload one PDF per filled form or add each form's photos by hand.
4. Leave **Keep each map with its tables on one page** on unless you are short of paper: a map that does not fit under the questions above it then moves to the next page whole, so its table of marks is never cut in two.
5. Press **Download PDF**. A draft survey prints with a DRAFT watermark, which is fine for proofing; publish it before handing sheets out.
6. Print the PDF at 100 % scale, in colour or black and white. Do not fit to page: the four black squares in the corners must stay 8 mm from the edges.

## What is on the form

- **Questions** in the order respondents see them, with the help text. Choices are boxes, text answers are lines, ratings and scales are numbered boxes. A question that respondents see only after a certain answer carries an "Answer only if …" line. Questions set to **Operators only** under *Who sees this* are left off.
- **The map** of every map page, with your reference layers drawn on it and a scale bar. Under it, a legend gives each map question a letter: **A ●** for a point, **B ━** for a line, **C ▭** for an area. The respondent draws the symbol on the map and writes the letter next to it. The follow-up questions of a map question are a small table with a row per mark (A1, A2, …).
- **A code on every page**: two QR codes in the bottom corners and a line between them, such as `MS-7K · #37 · Page 3 of 4`. It names this printed form, the copy and the page, so a photographed page is matched to the right survey version, response and map. A numbered copy also shows its number large at the top right. Keep the pages whole; a page without its code cannot be matched. A photocopy of a numbered copy repeats its number, so print more copies instead.
- **On page 1**, a QR code with the survey's online link for people who would rather answer on a phone, and an "Office use" box for the sheet number you give each filled sheet.

## After the event

Bring the filled forms back with **Enter a response** on the Responses page, which is part of Pro: photograph or scan them and check what was read ([Enter paper forms from photos](/how-to/enter-paper-forms-from-photos/)), or type them in ([Enter responses for others](/how-to/enter-responses-for-others/)).
