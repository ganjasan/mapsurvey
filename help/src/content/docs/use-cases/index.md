One page per real case — a geography class mapping its neighbourhood, a town asking where to put a crossing, a study of how people name regions. Each page says what was asked, how the survey was built, what came back, and what we would change. The full stories, with pictures, are on [mapsurvey.org](https://mapsurvey.org/#stories).
